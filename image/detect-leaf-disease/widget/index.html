Leaf Doctor — bean crop check

Farmers photograph a bean leaf in the field and get an answer straight away: healthy, angular leaf spot, or bean rust.

The chat on the right is the PlaxorStack widget in inline mode, with photo upload turned on. Attach one of the pictures from data/try/ and ask "What's wrong with this leaf?"

To make it work, replace YOUR_WIDGET_KEY below with a Widget key for your leaf model, and serve this folder from a local web server (npx serve widget). See the example's README, step 6.
